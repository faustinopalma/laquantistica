Accumula misure: la pila cresce solo sui multipli interi di e — è la quantizzazione della carica.Adjust the voltage with the slider until the drop is suspended (green), then press MEASURE: the value flies into the chart and a new drop appears at once. Accumulate measurements: the stack grows only at integer multiples of e — charge quantization.
