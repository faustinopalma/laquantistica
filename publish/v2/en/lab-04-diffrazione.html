Each electron arrives as a localized dot (particle), yet many dots build up (wave): this is wave–particle duality. A polycrystalline sample (randomly oriented crystallites) gives rings; a single crystal gives a lattice of spots (a projection of the reciprocal lattice). Raise the voltage → smaller λ → tighter pattern (∝ 1/√V). The classical model would give only a central blob.
