Non conta sospendere una goccia, ma accumulare misure che rivelano la quantizzazione.Controls on the model: click the atomizer to let a droplet in, use the slider under the apparatus to adjust the field, then press MEASURE: the value flies into the histogram. The goal is not to suspend one drop, but to accumulate measurements revealing quantization.
