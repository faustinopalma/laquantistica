Choose a filter and press ILLUMINATE: the photons tear electrons out of the cathode, they reach the ring and charge the capacitor. As the voltage rises the electrons are pushed back, until they all stop: the final voltage V0 measures their energy. Change the iris: the current and the charging time change, V0 does not. Change the colour: V0 changes. The four filters marked ● are those measured in the thesis. The least-squares line gives the slope h/e, the work function and the threshold frequency. With the ordinary voltmeter the current flows back through the instrument and the capacitor never reaches V0: an impedance buffer is needed. With the classical wave model the energy would depend on the intensity and not on the frequency: that is not what is observed.
