We want to measure the ratio e/m between the charge and the mass of the beam particles. It takes two steps: first the two fields are balanced, and the equilibrium gives the speed v = E/B; then the electric field is switched off and the deflection due to the magnetic field alone is measured.

The reading on the screen is taken by eye on the graduated scale, so it is rounded to half a millimetre: that is where the uncertainty in the result comes from. If the deflection is too strong the beam hits the plates and never reaches the screen: then Va must be raised or the fields reduced.
