Measuring m₀:

Each branch alone gives P(m₀=−k)=50%. One would think opening both branches adds up to 100%: instead it cancels to 0% (|m₀=+k⟩ is rebuilt). Blocking both branches, obviously, lets nothing through: 0%.

50% + 50% → 0%: amplitudes add, not probabilities.
