Press SWEEP: the voltage VBC rises slowly and the curve I(V) is drawn on the oscilloscope. Classically the current should only grow; instead, at regular intervals it drops: the electrons that reach the energy of the first level hand it over wholesale to the atoms and can no longer overcome the brake VCD. Each drop adds a luminous zone between the grids — neon shows it in red, mercury emits in the ultraviolet and nothing is seen. The spacing of the maxima measures the level: ~19 V for neon, ~5 V for mercury. Switch to the classical model for contrast: a monotonic curve, a dark bulb.
